Each state

  • Scheduled, with its payment record.
  • Live, with Pause.
  • Ended after a two-day pause, and its report.

Try the whole flow

  • Create a campaign that starts tomorrow. Once the payment record is filled in, it's listed under Scheduled.
  • Select View on its row to see its details and payment record.
  • Use Demo time at the top of the page to move forward a day. It goes live and appears in Discover.
  • Pause it from the campaign list, move forward a day or two, then resume it. While it's paused it isn't in Discover, and the paused days are added to its end date.
  • Move forward until the package ends. It moves to Ended and its PDF report becomes available.
  • Press Reset to start again. Your changes stay in your own browser.

What's real and what isn't

  • Prices, durations, prime-time hours, payment methods and creative specs are placeholders. So are the sample payment records.
  • Advertisers and results are made up. Results are simulated, plus any views and clicks you make here. Countries on the report are placeholders.
  • The report shows a mock of the ad in the feed where a real report would have a screenshot.
  • The Discover feed and video screen follow the Figma design. Figma has no admin side, so the admin layout is a proposal.
  • The enquiry form sends nothing.
