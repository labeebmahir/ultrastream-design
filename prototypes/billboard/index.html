Try the whole flow

  • Create a campaign that starts tomorrow, then find it under Scheduled.
  • Use Demo time at the top of the page to move forward a day. It goes live and appears in Discover.
  • Move forward until the package ends. It moves to Ended and its PDF report becomes available.
  • Press Reset to start again. Your changes stay in your own browser.

What's real and what isn't

  • Package names, prices, durations, prime-time hours and creative specs are placeholders.
  • Advertisers and results are made up. Results are simulated, plus any views and clicks you make here.
  • The Discover feed and video screen follow the Figma design. Figma has no admin side, so the admin layout is a proposal.
  • The enquiry form sends nothing.
